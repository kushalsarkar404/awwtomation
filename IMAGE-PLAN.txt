AWWTOMATION IMAGE PROMPTS
=========================

Two rules:
  1. Photos in public/site-assets are page photography. Photos in public/mockups
     go inside the phone mock-ups. Never crop one into the other. That is the
     mistake on the site today: the avatars are cut out of the hero photos.
  2. Every prompt ends with the house style line below. Paste it in full.

HOUSE STYLE (append to every prompt):
  Natural available light, soft warm colour grade, shallow depth of field,
  candid documentary feel, real skin texture, no retouching gloss, shot on a
  35mm lens. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no visible phone or app screen.

THE CAST (paste the matching description into any prompt that names a person):
  SITA RAI, 27, Nepali woman, clothing shop owner. Shoulder-length wavy dark
  brown hair, gold hoop earrings, mustard and rust knitwear, open smile.
  ANISHA GURUNG, 24, Nepali woman, customer. Straight black bob to the jaw,
  tiny gold nose stud, pastel shirts and a denim jacket, round face.
  AARAV SHRESTHA, 30, Nepali man, customer. Short trimmed beard, thick dark
  hair, olive utility jacket over a plain white tee.
  ROHAN KC, 26, Nepali man, creator. Loose curly hair, light stubble,
  oversized washed tees, a canvas camera bag on one shoulder.
  KIRAN THAPA, 25, Nepali woman, creator. Long dark hair in a loose top bun,
  thin wire-frame glasses, layered earrings, oversized linen shirts.
  BIKRAM TAMANG, 36, Nepali man, cafe owner. Close-cropped salt-and-pepper
  hair, checked shirt with rolled sleeves, dark canvas apron, laugh lines.
