Share the inbox, automations and contacts with your team. Invites are links you send yourself, so they work over WhatsApp, Viber or anywhere else.

## Steps

### Open Team settings

Open the account menu, go to **Settings** and click the **Team** tab. You'll see everyone in the organization, their role and how many seats your plan has. Click **Invite member**.

![Team settings with the Invite member button](/how-to/invite-your-team/1-team.webp "app.awwtomation.com/settings/team")

### Enter their email and role

Type the email they sign in to Google with, then pick a **Role**. **Member** builds automations, uses the inbox and manages contacts. **Admin** can also manage the team and billing. Click **Create invite link**.

![The Invite a teammate dialog with an email and the Member role](/how-to/invite-your-team/2-invite.webp "app.awwtomation.com/settings/team")

### Send them the link

No email is sent. Click **Copy** and send the link however you like. It only works for that email address and expires in 7 days.

![Share the invite link, with the Copy button](/how-to/invite-your-team/3-share-link.webp "app.awwtomation.com/settings/team")

### Keep an eye on pending invites

Open invites wait under **Pending invitations** and hold a seat until they're accepted. **Copy link** sends it again; the bin revokes it.

![Team settings listing two pending invitations](/how-to/invite-your-team/4-pending.webp "app.awwtomation.com/settings/team")

## Q: How many people can I invite?

A: One seat on Starter, five on Pro and twenty on Agency, including you.

## Q: Can someone work across several brands?

A: Yes. An organization can hold several workspaces, one per brand or client, and your team switches between them from the workspace menu.
